## Original game

Flying Rush was first made in 2021 with Core by Manticore Games, on Core's "Deathmatch"
framework. The rules, the map's layout and the idea come from that version; its files (Core's
framework scripts, community content such as a railgun, music and shell-ejection effects made
for Core, and the publish data) were removed from the working tree when the game was rebuilt
in Unity and remain only in the git history. None of them is used by this project, and no
Core or Manticore Games branding is part of it. See [docs/original](docs/original/README.md).
